# Saka 2.1.2

The blue scroll thumb is wider and visually 30% shorter, with a slightly darker blue gradient, rounded edges and a soft highlight. Its full drag target is preserved. Smooth wheel scrolling and direct thumb dragging are unchanged.

The mascot keeps its approved artwork. Transparent margins are cropped at display time so the character uses the right-hand space more effectively, with a wider allowance on large windows. High-quality resampling and pixel-aligned layout are retained, and the app now declares per-monitor DPI awareness. This is a rendering and layout update, not an AI upscale or a replacement drawing.
